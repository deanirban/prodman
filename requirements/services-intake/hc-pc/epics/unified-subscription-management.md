# [HC-PC] Unified Subscription Management for HPE GreenLake Flex

## Summary

Enable customers to manage all HC-PC service subscriptions from a single unified
view within the HPE GreenLake platform, reducing operational overhead and
improving subscription lifecycle visibility.

## Business Value

- Reduces time-to-manage subscriptions by 40%
- Eliminates need to navigate multiple portals for subscription actions
- Provides consolidated renewal and capacity planning views

## Acceptance Criteria

- Customer can view all active HC-PC subscriptions in one dashboard
- Subscription renewal, upgrade, and cancellation actions are available inline
- Notifications trigger 90/60/30 days before expiry
- Export to CSV available for all subscription data
