# [HC-PC] Self-Service Hardware Refresh Workflow

## Summary

Enable HC-PC customers to initiate and track hardware refresh cycles
self-service within HPE GreenLake, reducing dependency on HPE support
teams for routine refresh requests.

## Business Value

- Cuts average refresh initiation time from 5 days to same-day
- Reduces HPE support ticket volume for refresh requests by 60%
- Provides customers full visibility into refresh status and timelines

## Acceptance Criteria

- Customer can submit a hardware refresh request from the GreenLake portal
- Request automatically routes to the correct HPE fulfilment team
- Customer receives real-time status updates at each stage
- Completed refresh is reflected in inventory within 24 hours
