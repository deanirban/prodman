# [HC-PC] Automated License Compliance Reporting

## Summary

Provide automated compliance reporting for HC-PC software licenses within the
HPE GreenLake platform, enabling customers to track license usage against
entitlements and receive proactive alerts on compliance gaps.

## Business Value

- Eliminates manual license audits saving 8+ hours per quarter per customer
- Reduces compliance risk through real-time entitlement tracking
- Supports audit-readiness with exportable compliance reports

## Acceptance Criteria

- Dashboard shows current license utilization vs entitlement per product
- Alerts trigger when utilization exceeds 80%, 90%, and 100% thresholds
- Monthly compliance summary exported automatically to customer email
- Drill-down view shows per-device license assignment
